A few things
to know.

Still have a question? Give us a ring.

Do I need an appointment?

Calling ahead is the best way to check availability and arrange a time. Walk-in availability can vary, so we recommend getting in touch before visiting.

Do you offer skin fades and beard trims?

Yes. Haircuts, fades, tapers and beard grooming are part of our service selection. Get in touch if you're after a specific finish.

Where can I find your prices?

For up-to-date pricing, please contact the shop directly at +44 7842 614388.

Are you open on Sundays?

Our published Sunday hours are 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Hours may change on public holidays, so check by phone before travelling.
