Privacy information.

This Royal Barber Shop website presents public business information and contact options. No account is required and this website does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Booking enquiries

When you fill in the booking-enquiry form, your information stays in your browser until you choose to send it through your own email application. The website does not automatically transmit or store the form data on a server. If you email the shop, your email provider and the receiving mailbox will process that message under their respective privacy practices.

External services

We load fonts from Google Fonts, illustrative photography from Unsplash and an embedded Google Map. Visiting these sections may connect your browser to those providers. Our social media and map links open external websites with their own privacy practices.
